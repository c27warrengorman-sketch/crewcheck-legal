CrewCheck

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

CrewCheck ("we," "our," or "us") operates a crew management platform that helps small service businesses coordinate field crews and communicate job completion to their customers. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When a business owner registers for CrewCheck, we collect their name, email address, phone number, and business name. When crew members are invited, we collect their name and phone number.

Property & Customer Information: Business owners enter customer property addresses, customer names, and customer phone numbers into the Service. This information is provided by the business owner and used solely for service coordination and job completion reporting.

Job Data: We collect information related to jobs created within the Service, including job descriptions, checklist items and completion status, crew notes, and timestamps.

Photos: Crew members upload photos through the Service. Photos are captured with device GPS coordinates and timestamps to verify job completion location and time.

Payment Information: Business owners may enter their Venmo handle, Zelle phone/email, or CashApp tag to display on customer-facing job reports. CrewCheck does not process payments, handle financial transactions, or store credit card or bank account information.

Device Information: We collect device identifiers for push notification delivery and GPS coordinates at the time of photo capture for verification purposes.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the CrewCheck platform
  • Send SMS messages for crew invitations and job completion reports
  • Display job completion reports to customers via web links
  • Verify job completion through geotagged and timestamped photos
  • Send push notifications to crew members about job assignments
  • Improve and maintain the Service

3. SMS Messaging

CrewCheck sends SMS messages only after explicit opt-in consent is received from the end user via their mobile device. Users can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

CrewCheck sends two types of SMS messages:

Crew Invite Messages: A one-time message sent to crew members when invited by a business owner, containing a link to join the platform. Sent only after the crew member replies YES to an opt-in confirmation message.

Job Completion Reports: A message sent to customers when a service job is completed at their property, containing a link to view photos and details. Sent only after the customer replies YES to an opt-in confirmation message.

Message Frequency: Crew invite messages are sent once per crew member. Job completion messages are sent approximately once per service visit (typically 1–4 times per month for recurring service customers).

Opting Out: Recipients can reply STOP to any message to immediately opt out of future messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Job Completion Reports: When a business owner sends a job completion report, the customer can view the property address, job date, completion photos, and completed checklist items via a secure link. Customer personal information is not displayed on the report.
  • Service Providers: We use third-party services for SMS delivery (Twilio), cloud hosting, and file storage. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually required to protect it.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure cloud storage for photos and data, JWT-based authentication with token expiration, and role-based access controls ensuring crew members only see data relevant to their assigned jobs.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and job data for as long as the business owner's account is active. Business owners can request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us at support@crewcheck.app. Customer phone numbers associated with opted-out users are flagged and no further messages are sent.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us. Business owners can delete properties, crew members, and job data directly within the application.

8. Children's Privacy

CrewCheck is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
